

This repository contains an interactive Power BI dashboard focused on analyzing and visualizing **Employee Attrition Rates** using a provided HR dataset. The dashboard provides key insights into attrition trends, helping HR and business stakeholders make data-driven decisions to reduce employee turnover.

---

## 🧠 Objective

To explore, analyze, and visualize employee attrition patterns based on various demographic, performance, and job-related factors using Power BI. The goal is to understand:
- Which departments face the highest attrition
- What roles or experience levels are most vulnerable
- Impact of age, income, satisfaction levels, and work environment on attrition

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Overall Attrition Rate** (KPI Card)
- **Attrition by Department and Job Role**
- **Attrition vs Age, Gender, and Education**
- **Monthly Income vs Attrition**
- **Work-Life Balance and Job Satisfaction Metrics**
- **Years at Company / Years in Current Role**
- **Interactive filters** (slicers for gender, department, job role, etc.)

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- 💼 Power BI (Desktop)
- 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- 📄 CSV dataset (structured HR attrition data)
- 🧹 Power Query for data cleaning and transformation

---

## 📌 Insights Discovered

- Higher attrition in specific departments such as [e.g., Sales, R&D].
- Employees with low job satisfaction and low income are more likely to leave.
- Attrition is higher among younger employees or those with fewer years in the company.
- Work-life balance and overtime have a direct impact on attrition.
